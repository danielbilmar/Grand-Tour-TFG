Objectives

General objective

GOTo analyse the transformations and continuities of architectural travel as an educational experience from the Grand Tour to the second half of the twentieth century, through five case studies, assessing its initiatory character and its possible relationships with the later production of its protagonists.

Specific objectives

SO1To identify and characterise, on the basis of the literature, different types of architectural journey since the Grand Tour, considering their historical and artistic contexts, their characteristics and their possible coexistence.

SO2To select and justify a case study to explore each of the proposed types of journey, considering its typological representativeness, educational relevance, documentary availability and historiographical relevance.

SO3To gather and select the historical, spatial and chronological information on each journey, as well as the available graphic and written documentation, distinguishing documented data from estimates and hypotheses.

SO4To reconstruct and organise the itineraries in a Geographic Information System that makes it possible to represent and compare them.

SO5To analyse the possible relationships between the experiences of each journey and its protagonist’s later architectural production, assessing the documentary support for the influences identified.

SO6To synthesise the results of the case studies in order to identify changes and continuities in architectural travel as an educational experience.
