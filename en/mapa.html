Comparative map

The five routes overlaid on a single projection. Toggle each journey on or off, zoom with the scroll wheel or the buttons and hover over the points to see place and dates.

Overlaying the itineraries reveals the stretches repeated across cases —such as Soane’s and Le Corbusier’s routes through Italy, or the constant presence of Rome and Athens— and the progressive geographical expansion of the journey from the classical Grand Tour to Ando’s journey round the world.
